Database Performance & Reliability Engineering

What we deliver

Scope is confirmed in writing in the assessment, with anything out of scope listed too.

  1. 01

    Workload analysis

    Top queries, waits and locks from pg_stat_statements, Performance Schema, Query Store, AWR or the profiler.

  2. 02

    Query and index tuning

    Rewrites, indexes and plan fixes tested against production-like data.

  3. 03

    Configuration and sizing

    Memory, connections, storage and instance class tuned to the workload, often cutting cost too.

  4. 04

    High availability

    Replicas, Multi-AZ, Patroni, Always On, Data Guard or replica sets designed and failover-tested.

  5. 05

    Disaster recovery

    Cross-region or cross-cloud recovery with measured RTO and RPO.

  6. 06

    Restore testing

    Backups restored, timed and documented so you can show auditors.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Do you need production access?

Not for the review. Exports of statistics, slow-query logs and configuration are usually enough. We sign your NDA before we see anything.

Will tuning reduce our cloud bill?

Often. Fixing the top queries usually lets instances be right-sized. We report the expected saving with each change.

Do you test failover in production?

Only with your agreement and in a planned window. Most testing happens on a production-like copy first.

Is the review fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the remediation work if you go ahead.
