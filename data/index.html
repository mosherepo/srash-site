What is driving demand right now

Backed by public sources.

  1. Licence change

    Redis to Valkey

    Redis moved to source-available licences in 2024, then added AGPLv3 in Redis 8. Valkey, the Linux Foundation fork, stays BSD and costs less on ElastiCache.

    Redis, Valkey & Caching Source: Redis

  2. Extended-support fees

    Paying to stay on old versions

    Amazon RDS and Cloud SQL charge extended support on PostgreSQL and MySQL versions past their standard support date, and the charge rises over time.

    Database Upgrades & End-of-Support Source: AWS

How we deliver Data & Databases work

The same five phases on every engagement in this practice, so you always know what happens next and what you will hold at the end of each phase.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Inventory engines, versions, sizes, dependencies and support dates from read-only access or exports.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Compatibility, performance baselines, backup and restore proof, and a risk register.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Waves, windows, rollback and a fixed quote, in a written plan you keep.

  4. 04

    Rehearse

    Every change runs end to end in a production-like environment, with measured timings.

  5. 05

    Cut over and hand over

    Executed in your window, verified against agreed checks, documented in your repository.

Questions about Data & Databases

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Which database engines do you work with?

PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server and Oracle, their managed versions (Aurora, RDS, Azure SQL, Cloud SQL, AlloyDB, Spanner), and MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, Redis, Valkey, DynamoDB and Cosmos DB.

Are you a DBA agency?

No. We are an engineering firm. We upgrade, migrate, design and automate data platforms, and run them SRE-style when you want that, with a named senior engineer rather than a ticket queue.

Do you need production access?

Not for assessments. Read-only access or exports are usually enough, and we sign your NDA first.

Do you offer 24x7 support?

No. Managed services are business hours by default, with extended hours agreed in writing for specific windows.
