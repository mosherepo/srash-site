Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

When exactly does WebLogic 12c support end?

Oracle states that Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12c (12.2.1.4, or 12.2.1.19 for Forms and Reports) concludes in December 2026, and Extended Support ends in December 2027.

What happens to RDS Custom for Oracle after March 31, 2027?

AWS states you will no longer be able to access the RDS Custom for Oracle console or resources. AWS recommends self-managed Oracle on Amazon EC2; RDS for Oracle or Oracle Database@AWS may fit better for some databases.

Why 14.1.2?

Fusion Middleware 14.1.2 shipped in December 2024 and Oracle plans Premier Support through 2030 and Extended Support through 2033.

Are review fees credited?

Yes. Readiness review and exit plan fees are deducted in full from the fixed fee of the work if you go ahead.
