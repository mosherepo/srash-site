NoSQL Engineering: MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, DynamoDB & Cosmos DB

NoSQL clusters run quietly until a version leaves support, a partition gets hot or the bill doubles. We upgrade, re-model, migrate and tune MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, DynamoDB and Cosmos DB with the same discipline we bring to relational systems.

The challenge

Sound familiar?

  • MongoDB 6.0 is already out of support and 7.0 ends on August 31, 2027; you want to land on 8.0 without surprises.
  • Your Cassandra 3.11 ring has been unsupported since September 2024 and the upgrade must go one major version at a time.
  • Aerospike 7.x releases reached end of life during 2025 and 2026, and the team that built the cluster has moved on.
  • DynamoDB or Cosmos DB costs are climbing because the partition key and access patterns no longer match the product.

What you receive

  • Health check report with prioritized findings
  • Version path and rolling-upgrade runbook
  • Data model recommendations
  • Cost and capacity review
  • Monitoring, backup and restore tests

FAQ

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Can you upgrade Cassandra without downtime?

Yes, in most cases. Apache Cassandra supports online, rolling upgrades between adjacent major versions. We upgrade node by node with repairs and checks between each step.

Should we move from self-managed MongoDB to Atlas?

Often it pays back in reduced operations work, but not always. The health check compares the run cost and operating effort before you decide.

Do you work with DataStax or MongoDB directly?

We are independent and not a vendor partner. We work with your existing vendor support contracts where you have them.

Is the health check fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the project if you go ahead.
