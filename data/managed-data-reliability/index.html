Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Is this 24x7 support?

No. The service is business hours by default. Extended hours can be agreed in writing for specific windows, such as a migration weekend or go-live.

Who will we work with?

A named senior engineer who knows your estate, not a rotating queue.

Can we cancel?

Yes, with the notice period in the agreement. All runbooks and automation stay with you.

Which engines do you cover?

Relational, NoSQL and in-memory engines on premises and on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI. The onboarding confirms scope in writing.
