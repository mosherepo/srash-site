PostgreSQL fleet upgrade before Extended Support charges

A software company runs a few dozen Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL 13 and 14 instances. Standard support for 14 ends Feb 28, 2027 and Extended Support is billed per vCPU-hour after that.Illustrative · Cloud · Readiness review 1 week; upgrades 2–6 weeks

EKS clusters out of extended support

A company runs several EKS clusters on 1.31 and 1.32. Extended support costs more per cluster-hour and 1.31 extended support ends Nov 26, 2026, after which clusters are auto-upgraded.Illustrative · AI · Readiness assessment 2 weeks; hardening 4–8 weeks
