01 · Data practice

Data & Databases

We upgrade, migrate, tune and run the databases your products depend on, from PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server and Oracle to MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, Redis and Valkey, DynamoDB and Cosmos DB. Every engagement is fixed fee with a written plan.

Practice overview

  1. 01Database Upgrades & End-of-SupportUpgrade PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Cassandra and Aerospike before support ends and extended-support fees pile up.
  2. 02Database Migration & ModernizationLicence exits and consolidation: Oracle and SQL Server to PostgreSQL, Aurora, AlloyDB, Azure SQL or Cloud SQL, validated row by row.
  3. 03NoSQL EngineeringUpgrades, Atlas and Astra DB moves, data modelling and cost tuning for MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, DynamoDB and Cosmos DB.
  4. 04Redis, Valkey & CachingRedis or Valkey after the licence change, ElastiCache and Memorystore moves, and Azure Cache for Redis before it retires.
  5. 05Performance & ReliabilityFind the real bottlenecks, test failover and prove your backups restore, across relational and NoSQL engines.
  6. 06Managed Data ReliabilityA named senior engineer runs your data platforms SRE-style: SLOs, patching, tested restores and a monthly report.
  7. 07Oracle & Middleware ModernizationWebLogic 12c to 14.1.2 before Dec 2026, RDS Custom for Oracle exits before Mar 31, 2027, 19c and 26ai upgrades.

02 · Cloud practice

Cloud & Platform Engineering

We build secure landing zones, migrate workloads off data centres and VMware, run Kubernetes upgrades on EKS, AKS, GKE and OKE, automate delivery with CI/CD and Terraform, and cut cloud bills with evidence instead of guesswork.

Practice overview

  1. 08Landing Zones, Security & GovernanceSecure, well-governed foundations on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI, built as Terraform you own.
  2. 09Cloud Migration & VMware ExitData centre and VMware exits with an honest portfolio assessment, a run-cost model and rehearsed migration waves.
  3. 10Kubernetes & Platform EngineeringKeep EKS, AKS, GKE and OKE clusters supported, and build platforms developers actually like using.
  4. 11DevOps, IaC & ObservabilityCI/CD, Terraform or OpenTofu, GitOps and OpenTelemetry observability that answers questions during an incident.
  5. 12FinOps & Cost OptimizationCut cloud, database and AI spend with evidence, then keep it down with tagging, budgets and a monthly review.

03 · AI practice

AI & Automation

We design vector search on the database you already run, build retrieval-augmented assistants and agentic workflows, ship GenAI features into your products, and put evaluation, guardrails and cost controls around all of it.

Practice overview

  1. 13Vector Databases & SearchChoose and run the right vector store, from pgvector and Atlas to Pinecone, Weaviate and Milvus, with measured relevance.
  2. 14RAG & Knowledge AssistantsAssistants grounded in your documents and data, with citations, permission-aware retrieval and evaluation.
  3. 15AI Agents & Agentic WorkflowsAgentic workflows that call your systems through scoped tools and MCP, with human approvals and audit trails.
  4. 16GenAI App DevelopmentGenAI features, copilots and document AI built into your products as tested, monitored software.
  5. 17LLMOps, Evaluation & GuardrailsEvaluation suites, guardrails, tracing and cost controls that get GenAI pilots signed off for production.
  6. 18Intelligent AutomationReliable workflows with AI at the right steps: document processing, request routing and ops runbooks.
