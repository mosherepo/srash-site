Work with us

A network of senior independent engineers.

We work with experienced independent engineers across data, cloud and AI on a project basis. If that is you, we would like to hear from you.

What we look for

  • Long production experience in at least one of: relational or NoSQL databases, cloud platforms and Kubernetes, or applied AI engineering
  • Comfort owning an engagement end to end, from assessment to hand-over
  • Clear writing: plans, runbooks and documentation clients can keep
  • Discipline with access, data and change control (see our practices)

How it works

  • Project-based contracts
  • Same NDA, security and data-handling terms as our own engineers
  • Remote, with occasional on-site cut-overs

Email hello@srash.io with the subject “Engineer network”, your specialties and a short summary of recent work. Please do not send client-confidential material.
