Real-time Data Streaming & Change Data Capture

Overnight batch loads cannot feed fraud checks, live inventory or an assistant that needs today's data. We design and build streaming and change-data-capture pipelines from your operational databases into the lakehouse and real-time stores, with schemas, ordering and replay handled from the start.

Sound familiar?

  • Nightly batch loads arrive too late for the business, and full-table extracts load the source databases.
  • Kafka clusters still run on ZooKeeper, and Apache Kafka 4.0 removed ZooKeeper support.
  • CDC from Oracle, SQL Server or PostgreSQL breaks on schema changes, and nobody trusts the target to match the source.
  • Streams land in the lake as small files and duplicates, and the cost keeps growing.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Do we really need real time?

Often not for everything. We separate the few flows that need seconds from the many that are fine every hour, because streaming costs more to build and run.

What does Kafka 4.0 change for us?

Kafka 4.0 removed ZooKeeper mode. Clusters still on ZooKeeper move to KRaft through the 3.9 bridge release before they can upgrade. We plan and rehearse that path.

Will CDC slow down our production database?

Log-based CDC reads the transaction log rather than querying tables, so the extra load is usually small. We measure it on a copy first and size the connectors to suit.

Managed or self-run Kafka?

Confluent Cloud, Amazon MSK and Event Hubs remove most of the operations work; self-run Kafka can cost less at scale. The review compares both on your volumes.
