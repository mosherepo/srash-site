Data Warehouse to Lakehouse Migration

Warehouse migrations are usually estimated from a table count and then run late on the stored procedures, ETL jobs and reports nobody listed. We inventory what is really used, convert with tooling plus engineering review, run old and new side by side and cut over only when the numbers match.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Do automated converters do the whole job?

No. Tools such as Lakebridge and SnowConvert speed up routine SQL, but procedural code, macros and edge cases need an engineer to confirm the behaviour. We plan for both and report what was converted by tool and what by hand.

How do you prove the numbers match?

Each wave runs old and new side by side. Row counts, aggregates and key hashes are compared automatically, and business owners sign off the reports they rely on before cut-over.

Can Snowflake and Databricks share the same data?

Yes, through open table formats such as Apache Iceberg. The readiness sprint shows whether running both is worth the extra complexity for you.

What about Power BI Premium capacity?

Microsoft is retiring Power BI Premium P SKUs at the end of each agreement term and points customers to Fabric F SKUs. We include the capacity move in the plan when it applies.
