Data Quality, Governance & Observability

Data teams lose trust one silent failure at a time. We put tests, contracts, lineage and access policies around the data that matters most, so problems are caught before a dashboard or a model uses them, and auditors get clear answers.

Sound familiar?

  • Nobody can say who has access to sensitive columns, or where a number in a board report came from.
  • Upstream schema changes break pipelines and dashboards without warning.
  • A Hive metastore or legacy permissions need to move to Unity Catalog, and the access model was never designed.
  • A data quality tool was bought, but nobody wrote tests for the tables that matter.

What we deliver

Scope is confirmed in writing in the assessment, with anything out of scope listed too.

  1. 01

    Critical data inventory

    The tables, metrics and feeds that drive revenue, compliance and AI, each with an agreed owner.

  2. 02

    Access model

    Unity Catalog, Snowflake Horizon or Microsoft Purview roles, tags, row filters and column masks designed and applied as code.

  3. 03

    Quality tests

    Freshness, volume, schema and business-rule tests with dbt or Great Expectations, run on every load.

  4. 04

    Data contracts

    Agreed schemas and expectations between producing and consuming teams, checked in CI.

  5. 05

    Lineage and catalogue

    Column-level lineage and a searchable catalogue, so people find and trust the right tables.

  6. 06

    Observability

    Alerts on freshness, volume and distribution changes using platform features or tools such as Monte Carlo or Soda, routed to owners.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Do we need a separate data catalogue product?

Not always. Unity Catalog, Snowflake Horizon and Microsoft Purview cover much of what teams need. We start with what your platform already includes.

Can you migrate our Hive metastore to Unity Catalog?

Yes. We design catalogs, schemas and grants first, then migrate tables and permissions in waves, with access tested before and after.

Will governance slow our teams down?

Done well, it speeds them up: people find the right data, and access requests follow a clear rule instead of a ticket queue.

Do you help with compliance audits?

We document access, lineage and controls so your compliance team can answer auditors. We do not certify compliance ourselves.
