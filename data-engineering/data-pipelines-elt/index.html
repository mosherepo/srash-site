Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Should we buy connectors or build them?

Managed connectors are usually cheaper than engineering time for common SaaS sources; high-volume databases and unusual APIs are often cheaper to build. The health check compares both for your sources.

Do you only work with dbt?

dbt is our default for SQL transformation because it brings tests, documentation and lineage, but we also work with Spark, Databricks notebooks, Fabric and stored procedures where they fit better.

Airflow or Dagster?

Both work well. Airflow is the most widely known; Dagster models data assets directly. We usually keep what you run and fix how it is used, unless there is a clear reason to switch.

Can you take over pipelines a previous contractor built?

Yes. That is a common starting point, and the health check documents what exists before anything changes.
