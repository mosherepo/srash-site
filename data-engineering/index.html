Point of view

A data platform earns trust one correct number at a time. We build pipelines, models and platforms as tested code, reconcile every migration against the old system, and watch freshness, quality and cost on every run, so the business stops checking the numbers by hand.

8 services, each with a fixed-fee entry point.

Snowflake, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric. Pipelines, streaming, BI, governance and AI-ready data. Each is scoped after a free 30-minute review, and the assessment fee is credited to the project.

01 / 08

Lakehouse & Data Platform Build on Snowflake, Databricks or Microsoft Fabric

A data platform is a set of decisions about storage, compute, governance and cost. We make them with you, build them as code on Snowflake, Databricks or Microsoft Fabric, and put a first end-to-end data product into production so the platform proves itself early.

Sound familiar?

  • You have chosen Snowflake, Databricks or Fabric, but nobody has designed the environments, access model or cost controls.
  • Data lands in a lake nobody trusts: no bronze, silver and gold layers, no owners and no tests.
  • Every team built its own pipelines and warehouse, and the numbers do not match across dashboards.

You receive

  • Platform architecture and decision records
  • Terraform and CI/CD in your repository
  • Governance model with roles and policies

02 / 08

Data Warehouse to Lakehouse Migration

Warehouse migrations are usually estimated from a table count and then run late on the stored procedures, ETL jobs and reports nobody listed. We inventory what is really used, convert with tooling plus engineering review, run old and new side by side and cut over only when the numbers match.

Sound familiar?

  • A Teradata, Netezza or Exadata renewal or hardware refresh is coming and finance wants the move to the cloud costed.
  • Hadoop or Cloudera clusters still run Hive, Impala, Oozie and Sqoop jobs that the people who wrote them no longer maintain.
  • Azure Synapse or Power BI Premium capacity needs a plan now that Microsoft points customers to Fabric.

You receive

  • Inventory with every object in scope, retired or owned
  • Target architecture and wave plan
  • Converted code in your repository

Warehouse to Lakehouse Migration Starts with: Migration readiness sprint

03 / 08

Data Pipelines & ELT Engineering

Pipelines fail quietly: a late job, a renamed column, a dashboard still showing yesterday. We rebuild ingestion, transformation and orchestration as tested, version-controlled code, so data arrives on time and someone knows when it does not.

Sound familiar?

  • Hundreds of scheduled jobs, stored procedures and scripts that only one person understands.
  • Dashboards show stale or wrong numbers and the business notices before the data team does.
  • Connector or warehouse bills grew faster than the data, and nobody knows which pipeline drives the cost.

You receive

  • Pipeline inventory with cost and failure data
  • dbt project with tests and documentation
  • Orchestration code and schedules

Data Pipelines & ELT Starts with: Pipeline health check

04 / 08

Real-time Data Streaming & Change Data Capture

Overnight batch loads cannot feed fraud checks, live inventory or an assistant that needs today's data. We design and build streaming and change-data-capture pipelines from your operational databases into the lakehouse and real-time stores, with schemas, ordering and replay handled from the start.

Sound familiar?

  • Nightly batch loads arrive too late for the business, and full-table extracts load the source databases.
  • Kafka clusters still run on ZooKeeper, and Apache Kafka 4.0 removed ZooKeeper support.
  • CDC from Oracle, SQL Server or PostgreSQL breaks on schema changes, and nobody trusts the target to match the source.

You receive

  • Streaming architecture and decision records
  • CDC and streaming pipelines as code
  • Schema registry and data contracts

Real-time Streaming & CDC Starts with: Streaming architecture review

Analytics Engineering & BI: Power BI, Tableau and Looker

When three dashboards show three different revenue numbers, the problem is rarely the chart. We build the models, metric definitions and semantic layer underneath and then the dashboards on top, so every number has one definition and an owner.

Sound familiar?

  • Every team calculates revenue, churn or margin differently, and meetings start with an argument about the numbers.
  • Power BI or Tableau reports query raw tables directly and get slower every month.
  • Power BI Premium P SKUs are being retired at the end of each agreement term, and the move to Fabric capacity needs a plan.

You receive

  • Metric catalogue with owners
  • dbt models and semantic layer in your repository
  • Production dashboards with row-level security

06 / 08

Data Quality, Governance & Observability

Data teams lose trust one silent failure at a time. We put tests, contracts, lineage and access policies around the data that matters most, so problems are caught before a dashboard or a model uses them, and auditors get clear answers.

Sound familiar?

  • Nobody can say who has access to sensitive columns, or where a number in a board report came from.
  • Upstream schema changes break pipelines and dashboards without warning.
  • A Hive metastore or legacy permissions need to move to Unity Catalog, and the access model was never designed.

You receive

  • Critical data inventory with owners
  • Access model and policies as code
  • Quality test suite and data contracts

Snowflake & Databricks Cost Optimization

Consumption pricing rewards good engineering and punishes forgotten warehouses, oversized clusters and full reloads. We find where your credits and DBUs go, fix the biggest drivers first and put guardrails in place so the bill stays down.

Sound familiar?

  • The Snowflake or Databricks bill doubled while the data and the users did not.
  • Nobody can say which team, pipeline or dashboard drives the spend.
  • Warehouses and clusters run all night, oversized, with auto-suspend and auto-termination switched off.

You receive

  • Spend breakdown by team, workload and job
  • Savings register with each change and its effect
  • Tuned warehouses, clusters, queries and pipelines

AI-ready Data: Feature Pipelines, ML and RAG Data Engineering

Most AI projects stall on data, not models: the right documents are scattered, features are rebuilt in notebooks and nobody knows which version a model saw. We build the governed pipelines that feed models and assistants, with freshness, permissions and lineage carried through.

Sound familiar?

  • An AI pilot worked on a hand-picked sample, and nobody knows how to feed it production data.
  • Data scientists rebuild the same features in notebooks, and training and serving disagree.
  • Documents for a RAG assistant live in shared drives, wikis and tickets with mixed permissions.

You receive

  • AI data map per use case
  • Governed data products with tests
  • Feature and document pipelines as code

What is driving demand right now

Backed by public sources.

  1. Capacity change

    Power BI Premium P SKUs retiring

    Microsoft is retiring Power BI Premium per-capacity P SKUs at the end of each agreement term and moving customers to Fabric F SKUs. Capacity, regions and refresh schedules need a plan.

    Analytics Engineering & BI Source: Microsoft Learn

  2. Platform upgrade

    Kafka 4.0 removed ZooKeeper

    Apache Kafka 4.0 runs only in KRaft mode. Clusters still on ZooKeeper move to KRaft through the 3.9 bridge release before they can upgrade.

    Real-time Streaming & CDC Source: Apache Kafka

  3. Security change

    Snowflake ends password-only sign-in

    Snowflake is phasing out single-factor password sign-in, including for service users. Pipelines and tools that log in with a password need key-pair or OAuth authentication.

    Data Pipelines & ELT Source: Snowflake

How we deliver Data Engineering & Analytics work

The same five phases on every engagement in this practice, so you always know what happens next and what you hold at the end of each phase.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Sources, pipelines, consumers, query history and the bill, through read-only access or exports.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Data quality, freshness, cost drivers and risks, ranked, with the platform options compared on your workloads.

  3. 03

    Design

    Target architecture, models, access and cost guardrails, written down as decisions with a fixed quote.

  4. 04

    Build and reconcile

    Pipelines and platform delivered as code in increments, every load tested and every migrated table reconciled.

  5. 05

    Hand over and measure

    Runbooks, alerts and training for your team, and the agreed measures reported against the baseline.

Outcomes we measure

How we frame the business case. We agree the baseline and the measure in the statement of work and report against them. We never quote invented benchmarks or other clients' results. Building the business case

  1. Data freshness

    Time from a change in the source to the number in the dashboard, measured against the freshness target agreed for each data product.

  2. Pipeline reliability

    Failed and late runs per week, and time to detect and fix them, before and after.

  3. Reconciliation

    Tables and reports matched between old and new platforms, with any difference explained and signed off before cut-over.

  4. Platform cost

    Credits, DBUs or capacity units per workload against the baseline agreed at the start, from your own usage data.

  5. Trust and access

    Critical tables with an owner, tests and lineage, and access granted through policy rather than one-off grants.

Questions about Data Engineering & Analytics

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Which data platforms do you work with?

Snowflake, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric, plus BigQuery, Redshift and Synapse, with dbt, Airflow, Dagster, Fivetran, Airbyte, Kafka, Flink, Power BI, Tableau and Looker.

Do you hold partner status with Snowflake or Databricks?

No. We are independent and resell no licences or credits. Our recommendation follows your workload, and we work alongside your vendor partner if you have one.

Can you provide a data engineer for our team?

Yes. A senior data engineer can join your team for a fixed term, contracted corp-to-corp, working in your tools and your sprint rhythm. You interview the engineer before you commit.

Where does our data live during the work?

In your own accounts. We work through read-only access or exports for assessments, and through your repositories and pipelines for delivery.
