Snowflake & Databricks Cost Optimization

Consumption pricing rewards good engineering and punishes forgotten warehouses, oversized clusters and full reloads. We find where your credits and DBUs go, fix the biggest drivers first and put guardrails in place so the bill stays down.

Sound familiar?

  • The Snowflake or Databricks bill doubled while the data and the users did not.
  • Nobody can say which team, pipeline or dashboard drives the spend.
  • Warehouses and clusters run all night, oversized, with auto-suspend and auto-termination switched off.
  • A capacity commitment renewal is coming and finance wants a defensible forecast.

What you receive

  • Spend breakdown by team, workload and job
  • Savings register with each change and its effect
  • Tuned warehouses, clusters, queries and pipelines

What we deliver

Scope is confirmed in writing in the assessment, with anything out of scope listed too.

  1. 01

    Spend breakdown

    Credits, DBUs or capacity units attributed to warehouses, clusters, jobs, queries and teams from account usage and system tables.

  2. 02

    Compute right-sizing

    Warehouse sizes, multi-cluster settings, cluster policies, serverless choices and auto-suspend tuned to the real workload.

  3. 03

    Query and pipeline tuning

    The most expensive queries and jobs rewritten: incremental models instead of full reloads, pruning, clustering and caching.

  4. 04

    Storage and retention

    Time travel, fail-safe, snapshots, small files and unused tables reviewed and cleaned up.

  5. 05

    Guardrails

    Resource monitors, budgets, cluster policies, tagging and alerts so spend stays within agreed limits.

  6. 06

    Chargeback and forecast

    Cost by team or product, and a forecast that supports your next capacity commitment.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

How do you measure savings?

From your own bill and usage tables, comparing a baseline period with the period after each change, adjusted for workload growth where it can be measured. We do not invent percentages.

Do you take a share of the savings?

No. The health check and the sprint are fixed fee, so there is no incentive to cut things you need.

What access do you need?

Read access to account usage, system tables or billing exports. Changes are made by your team or by us under an agreed change process.

Will cost cuts slow down our dashboards?

Each change is tested against agreed query times and freshness targets before it is kept.
