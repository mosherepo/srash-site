Data & Databases services

Illustrative · Data & Databases

PostgreSQL fleet upgrade before Extended Support charges

Typical situation

A software company runs a few dozen Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL 13 and 14 instances. Standard support for 14 ends Feb 28, 2027 and Extended Support is billed per vCPU-hour after that.

Typical scope

  • Inventory of every instance, version, extension and parameter group
  • Extension and driver compatibility testing against the target version
  • Blue/green deployment rehearsal with measured switchover time
  • Wave plan grouped by risk and business window
Typical duration
Assessment 1–2 weeks; upgrade waves 4–8 weeks
Typical team
1 senior data engineer, plus your application owners for testing
Entry point
Upgrade readiness review (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Readiness report with a risk register
  • Tested runbook per wave, with rollback
  • Upgraded instances and a cost comparison before and after

Database Upgrades & End-of-Support

Illustrative · Data & Databases

Cassandra 3.11 and MongoDB 6.0 off end-of-life versions

Typical situation

A platform team still runs Cassandra 3.11 (end of life since Sep 2024) and MongoDB 6.0 (end of life since Jul 2025) behind customer-facing services.

Typical scope

  • Cluster health check: compaction, tombstones, repair, driver versions
  • Upgrade path without skipping majors (3.11 to 4.0 to 4.1; 6.0 to 7.0 to 8.0)
  • Rolling upgrade rehearsal in a production-like environment
  • Backup and restore proof before the first node is touched
Typical duration
Health check 1–2 weeks; rolling upgrades 3–6 weeks
Typical team
1 senior NoSQL engineer
Entry point
NoSQL health check & upgrade plan (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Health-check report with prioritized fixes
  • Rolling upgrade runbooks
  • Restore test evidence

Illustrative · Data & Databases

Redis to Valkey on ElastiCache

Typical situation

An engineering team pays for self-managed Redis and ElastiCache for Redis and wants to know whether Valkey is a safe, cheaper drop-in after the Redis licence change.

Typical scope

  • Command and module compatibility scan
  • Client library review and connection settings
  • Cost model: current spend against ElastiCache for Valkey rates
  • Cut-over plan with a dual-write or snapshot path
Typical duration
Review 1 week; migration 2–4 weeks
Typical team
1 senior data engineer
Entry point
Caching review & migration plan (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Compatibility and cost report
  • Migration runbook with rollback
  • Updated Terraform for the new clusters

Data Engineering & Analytics services

Illustrative · Data Engineering & Analytics

Teradata warehouse to a lakehouse before a hardware refresh

Typical situation

An enterprise analytics team runs a Teradata warehouse with BTEQ scripts and nightly ETL feeding finance and operations reports. A hardware refresh is due and leadership wants a cloud option costed before signing.

Typical scope

  • Inventory of tables, BTEQ scripts, macros, ETL jobs and report consumers, with unused objects marked
  • Target choice between Snowflake and Databricks on the actual query mix
  • Pilot conversion of the most complex subject area with tooling plus engineer review
  • Reconciliation approach and wave plan by subject area
Typical duration
Readiness sprint 3–4 weeks; migration waves per subject area
Typical team
1 senior data engineer and 1 analytics engineer, plus your report owners for sign-off
Entry point
Migration readiness sprint (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Inventory and retirement list
  • Target architecture and wave plan with a fixed quote
  • Pilot conversion with a reconciliation report

Warehouse to Lakehouse Migration

Illustrative · Data Engineering & Analytics

Snowflake spend review after the bill doubled

Typical situation

A software company's Snowflake bill grew much faster than its data and users. Warehouses were shared across teams, auto-suspend settings varied and dbt models rebuilt full tables every hour.

Typical scope

  • Credits attributed to warehouses, teams and dbt models from account usage views
  • Warehouse sizes, multi-cluster settings and auto-suspend reviewed against real query load
  • The most expensive models moved to incremental builds and tested
  • Resource monitors, budgets and tags defined as code
Typical duration
Health check 1–2 weeks; optimization sprint 3–4 weeks
Typical team
1 senior data engineer
Entry point
Data platform cost health check (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Spend breakdown by team and workload
  • Savings register with each change and its measured effect
  • Guardrails as code and a monthly cost view

Snowflake & Databricks Cost Optimization

Illustrative · Data Engineering & Analytics

Change data capture from PostgreSQL and SQL Server into the lakehouse

Typical situation

An operations team relied on nightly full extracts from PostgreSQL and SQL Server. Reports were a day late and the extracts slowed the production databases.

Typical scope

  • Latency needs agreed per table: seconds, minutes or daily
  • Debezium connectors on Kafka with a schema registry and an initial snapshot plan
  • Bronze to silver merge in the lakehouse with deduplication and ordering
  • Lag, throughput and row-count reconciliation dashboards
Typical duration
Architecture review 2 weeks; build 4–6 weeks
Typical team
1 senior data engineer, plus your database owners
Entry point
Streaming architecture review (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Streaming architecture and decision records
  • CDC pipelines as code with monitoring
  • Reconciliation and replay runbooks

Illustrative · Cloud & Platform Engineering

EKS clusters out of extended support

Typical situation

A company runs several EKS clusters on 1.31 and 1.32. Extended support costs more per cluster-hour and 1.31 extended support ends Nov 26, 2026, after which clusters are auto-upgraded.

Typical scope

  • Deprecated API scan across manifests and Helm charts
  • Add-on, CNI and ingress compatibility matrix
  • Blue/green node group strategy and control-plane upgrade rehearsal
  • GitOps changes so the next upgrade is routine
Typical duration
Readiness review 1 week; upgrades 2–6 weeks
Typical team
1 senior platform engineer
Entry point
Cluster upgrade readiness review (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Cluster inventory with support dates
  • Upgrade runbooks and rehearsal results
  • Pull requests in your repositories

AI & Automation services

Illustrative · AI & Automation

RAG assistant from pilot to production

Typical situation

A team built a promising internal assistant over policy documents, but answers are inconsistent, permissions are not enforced and nobody can say whether it is good enough to launch.

Typical scope

  • Evaluation set built with your subject-matter experts
  • Chunking, hybrid search and re-ranking experiments, measured
  • Permission-aware retrieval tied to your identity provider
  • Guardrails, tracing and cost limits
Typical duration
Readiness assessment 2 weeks; hardening 4–8 weeks
Typical team
1 senior AI engineer, plus a data engineer when needed
Entry point
RAG readiness assessment (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Evaluation report with baseline and target scores
  • Production architecture and runbook
  • Code and evaluation suite in your repository

Illustrative · AI & Automation

An operations agent with human approval

Typical situation

An operations team wants an agent that triages tickets and runs routine runbooks, but security will not allow unsupervised write access.

  • Human approval step for every change

Typical duration
Design sprint 2–3 weeks; build 4–6 weeks
Typical team
1 senior AI engineer

Agent design sprint (fixed fee)

What you hold at the end

  • Agent design and threat model
  • Working agent in a sandbox, then production
  • Approval and audit trail your security team can review
