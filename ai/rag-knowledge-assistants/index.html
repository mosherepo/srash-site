RAG & Enterprise Knowledge Assistants

A knowledge assistant is only useful if it answers from your sources, shows where the answer came from and respects who is allowed to see what. We build RAG systems that pass those tests, and measure them before your users do.

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Which model do you use?

Whichever fits your data residency, cost and quality needs: Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI or open models you host. The design keeps the model swappable.

Will our data be used to train a model?

Enterprise model services from the major clouds let you keep prompts and data out of training. We design for that and document it.

How do you stop wrong answers?

Grounding in retrieved sources, citations, refusal when sources are missing, and an evaluation suite run on every change.

Is the assessment fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the build if you go ahead.
