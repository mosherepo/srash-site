AI Agents & Agentic Workflows

Agents that take actions need more engineering than chatbots: permissions, approvals, logging and a way to stop. We build agentic workflows narrow enough to trust, measured against a clear business outcome.

Book a free 30-minute review →See pricing

The challenge

Sound familiar?

  • Leadership wants 'agents' and the team needs a use case that will survive contact with production.
  • A prototype works in a notebook but nobody knows how to give it safe access to real systems.
  • Risk and compliance need to know what the agent did, why, and who approved it.
  • Token costs and latency grow with every extra step the agent takes.

What you receive

  • Prioritized use-case shortlist
  • Agent and tool design

  • Evaluation suite and results
  • Production plan with controls and costs

FAQ

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Will the agent act without a human?

Only where you decide it should. Most first deployments keep people approving any action that changes data or spends money.

Why do so many agent projects fail?

Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, citing cost, unclear value and weak risk controls. We start with a measurable outcome, narrow scope and controls from day one.

Which framework do you use?

The one that fits your cloud and team. We favour designs where the model and framework can be swapped later.

Is the sprint fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the production build if you go ahead.
