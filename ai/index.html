Point of view

Most AI pilots stall on data, evaluation and cost, not on the model. We start from the data you already hold, measure answers before launch, and put guardrails and budgets around every call.

6 services, each with a fixed-fee entry point.

Vector search, RAG, AI agents, GenAI apps, LLMOps and automation on your own data. Each is scoped after a free 30-minute review, and the assessment fee is credited to the project.

Vector Databases & Semantic Search

The right vector store is often the database you already run. We help you choose between pgvector, MongoDB Atlas Vector Search, Redis, Oracle AI Vector Search and dedicated engines like Pinecone, Weaviate and Milvus, then build search that is measurably relevant.

Sound familiar?

  • Every vendor says their vector database is the answer and you need a neutral comparison.
  • Search results look plausible in a demo and wrong in production.
  • You already run PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis or Oracle and would rather not add another database to operate.

You receive

  • Engine comparison on your data
  • Ingestion and indexing pipeline
  • Hybrid search API

02 / 06

RAG & Enterprise Knowledge Assistants

A knowledge assistant is only useful if it answers from your sources, shows where the answer came from and respects who is allowed to see what. We build RAG systems that pass those tests, and measure them before your users do.

Sound familiar?

  • A chatbot pilot impressed in the demo, then gave confident wrong answers.
  • Content lives in SharePoint, Confluence, tickets, PDFs and databases, with different permissions everywhere.
  • Security will not approve anything that might show a document to the wrong person.

You receive

  • Architecture and evaluation plan
  • Working assistant with citations
  • Permission-aware retrieval

AI Agents & Agentic Workflows

Agents that take actions need more engineering than chatbots: permissions, approvals, logging and a way to stop. We build agentic workflows narrow enough to trust, measured against a clear business outcome.

Sound familiar?

  • Leadership wants 'agents' and the team needs a use case that will survive contact with production.
  • A prototype works in a notebook but nobody knows how to give it safe access to real systems.
  • Risk and compliance need to know what the agent did, why, and who approved it.

You receive

  • Prioritized use-case shortlist
  • Agent and tool design
  • Working prototype on sample data

How we deliver AI & Automation work

The same five phases on every engagement in this practice, so you always know what happens next and what you hold at the end of each phase.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Use case, users, data, risks and a measurable definition of success, agreed before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Evaluate first

    A test set and scoring built with your experts, so quality is measured rather than guessed.

  3. 03

    Build

    Retrieval, tools and prompts iterated against the evaluation, inside your cloud and model providers.

  4. 04

    Guard

    Permissions, guardrails, human approval where it matters, tracing and cost limits.

  5. 05

    Launch and hand over

    Monitored release, a runbook and the evaluation suite in your repository so you can keep improving.

Outcomes we measure

How we frame the business case. We agree the baseline and the measure in the statement of work and report against them. We never quote invented benchmarks or other clients' results. Building the business case

  1. Quality against threshold

    Score on an evaluation set built with your experts, against a pass mark agreed before building.

  2. Groundedness

    The share of answers that cite the right source, measured on the evaluation set.

  3. Cost per task

    Model and infrastructure cost per answer or per completed task, with daily limits.

  4. Time saved

    Time per task measured with real users in a pilot, not estimated from a vendor benchmark.

  5. Safety and access

    Permission-leak and prompt-injection tests passing, and the rate of human approvals and escalations.
