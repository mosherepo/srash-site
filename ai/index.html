How we deliver AI & Automation work

The same five phases on every engagement in this practice, so you always know what happens next and what you will hold at the end of each phase.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Use case, users, data, risks and a measurable definition of success, agreed before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Evaluate first

    A test set and scoring built with your experts, so quality is measured rather than guessed.

  3. 03

    Build

    Retrieval, tools and prompts iterated against the evaluation, inside your cloud and model providers.

  4. 04

    Guard

    Permissions, guardrails, human approval where it matters, tracing and cost limits.

  5. 05

    Launch and hand over

    Monitored release, a runbook and the evaluation suite in your repository so you can keep improving.

Ask a question

Which AI platforms do you use?

Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI, OpenAI, Anthropic and open models, with vector stores from pgvector and MongoDB Atlas to Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus, Redis and Oracle AI Vector Search.

Will our data be used to train models?

We design with enterprise model services and settings that keep your prompts and data out of training, and document how data flows.

How do you measure whether AI works?

Every build includes an evaluation suite with real questions or tasks, scored automatically on each change, plus human review.

Can you start small?

Yes. Most clients start with a fixed-fee assessment or design sprint on one use case.
