FAQ

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Can you work inside our existing product team?

Yes. We often embed a senior engineer with your team and hand the feature over to them.

Do you host models?

We design for your cloud. Managed model services are usually simplest; self-hosted open models make sense for some data or cost profiles.

How do you control cost?

Model choice, caching, prompt size limits and per-feature budgets with alerts.

Is the scoping fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the build if you go ahead.
