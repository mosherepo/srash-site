FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

What is the free 30-minute deadline check?

A video call with a senior engineer. We go through what you run, which support dates affect you and what a sensible next step would be. You leave with a short written summary. No slides, no obligation.

What does the paid review cost, and why is it paid?

Reviews are fixed price. The WebLogic readiness review is typically $4,500–$7,500 and the RDS Custom exit plan typically $7,500–$15,000. It is paid because it is real engineering work, and the full fee is credited toward the project if you go ahead.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price and fixed scope, agreed in writing before work starts. Changes are priced before they are made.

Do you need access to production?

No. Reviews work from inventory exports, AWR or pg_stat reports and configuration files you share. We sign your NDA before we see anything.

Who does the work?

A senior engineer on every engagement, from the first call to go-live.

Do you offer 24x7 support?

No. Managed services are business hours by default, with extended hours agreed in writing for specific windows such as a go-live.

How quickly will you reply?

Within one business day.
