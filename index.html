Why teams call us now

Licence changes, cost pressure and stalled pilots.

Not every trigger has a date. These are the ones we see most, each backed by a public source.

Licence change

Redis to Valkey

Redis moved to source-available licences in 2024, then added AGPLv3 in Redis 8. Valkey, the Linux Foundation fork, stays BSD and is priced lower on ElastiCache.

Redis, Valkey & Caching →Source: Redis

Extended-support fees

Paying to stay on old versions

Amazon RDS and Cloud SQL charge extended support on PostgreSQL and MySQL versions past their standard support date, and the charge rises over time.

Database Upgrades & End-of-Support →Source: AWS

Licence cost

VMware and database licence exits

Renewal shocks are pushing teams to move workloads and databases to cloud-native and open-source engines.

Cloud Migration & VMware Exit →Source: CloudBolt research

Cost pressure

Cloud and AI spend under review

Workload optimization and waste reduction lead the State of FinOps 2026, and AI cost is now in scope for most FinOps teams.

FinOps & Cost Optimization →Source: FinOps Foundation

Pilot to production

GenAI pilots that stalled

Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by the end of 2027 on cost, value or risk. Production needs evaluation, guardrails and cost control.

Engagement models

Buy an outcome, a service or senior capacity.

Pick the model that matches how your organization buys. All four come with the same commitments.

Fixed-price sprints

A defined outcome in 2–8 weeks, such as an upgrade, a landing zone or an agent prototype, for a price agreed in writing.

  • Written scope and plan
  • Rehearsal and rollback included
  • Changes priced before they are made

Best for deadlines and well-defined problems

Project delivery

Larger migrations, platforms and AI products delivered in fixed-price phases, each with its own scope and acceptance criteria.

  • Phased, fixed-price milestones
  • Weekly status and demo
  • Everything in your repository

Best for multi-month programs

Managed reliability

A named senior engineer runs your data or cloud platforms SRE-style for a fixed monthly fee.

  • Agreed SLOs and monitoring
  • Monthly operations and cost report
  • Business-hours cover by default

Best for teams without dedicated platform staff

Embedded senior engineers

Senior database, cloud, platform or AI engineers working inside your team, by the month.

  • Interview before you commit
  • Corp-to-corp contract
  • Weekly status and handover

Best for adding senior capacity fast

FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

What is the free 30-minute review?

A video call with a senior engineer about your databases, cloud platforms or AI plans. We cover what you run, which deadlines or cost issues affect you and what a sensible next step would be. You leave with a short written summary. No slides, no obligation.

What does the paid assessment cost, and why is it paid?

Assessments are fixed price, and most fall in a typical range of $4,000 to $15,000 depending on scope. Each offer page shows its own range. It is paid because it is real engineering work, and the full fee is credited toward the project if you go ahead.

Which technologies do you cover?

Relational and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, Redis, Valkey, DynamoDB, Cosmos DB), the four major clouds (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, OCI), Kubernetes and DevOps tooling, and AI stacks from vector databases to agents. See the platforms page for the full list.

Are you a partner of AWS, Microsoft, Google, Oracle or MongoDB?

No. We are independent. We do not resell licences or cloud capacity, so our recommendations are not tied to a commission.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price and fixed scope, agreed in writing before work starts. Managed services have a fixed monthly fee. Changes are priced before they are made.

Who does the work?

A senior engineer on every engagement, from the first call to go-live.

Do you offer 24x7 support?

No. Managed services are business hours by default, with extended hours agreed in writing for specific windows such as a go-live.

How quickly will you reply?

Within one business day.
