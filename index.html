Frequently asked questions

Is the 2-week assessment really free?

Yes, for a focused scope: one application estate or up to roughly 20 databases. You give us about two hours of your team’s time and read-only exports; we give you a written report you keep. Larger estates get a fixed-fee quote up front.

What do I get at the end of the assessment?

A written report with your inventory, support-deadline exposure, a keep / move / modernize decision for each system, a risk register, a cost comparison and a fixed price for the next step. It is yours whether or not you hire us.

Do you need access to our production systems?

No. We work from inventory exports, AWR or pg_stat reports and configuration files you share. Read-only access helps but is optional, and we sign your NDA.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price and fixed scope, agreed in writing before work starts. If we under-estimate, that is our problem, not yours.

Which clouds and databases do you work with?

AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. Oracle, PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, MongoDB, Cassandra, DynamoDB, Cosmos DB, Spanner and the main vector stores. We are independent and do not resell licences or cloud capacity.

We are on WebLogic or Forms 12.2.1.4. How urgent is this?

Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12.2.1.4 ends in December 2026 and Extended Support ends in December 2027. An upgrade to 14.1.2 usually takes 4 to 10 weeks, so it is worth starting now.

Do you work with companies outside the US?

Yes. We are based in San Diego, California and work remotely with teams in any time zone.
