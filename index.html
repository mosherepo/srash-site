Why teams call us now

Licence changes, cost pressure and stalled pilots. Not every trigger has a date; each of these is backed by a public source.

  1. Licence change

    Redis to Valkey

    Redis moved to source-available licences in 2024, then added AGPLv3 in Redis 8. Valkey, the Linux Foundation fork, stays BSD and costs less on ElastiCache.

    Redis, Valkey & Caching Source: Redis

  2. Extended-support fees

    Paying to stay on old versions

    Amazon RDS and Cloud SQL charge extended support on PostgreSQL and MySQL versions past their standard support date, and the charge rises over time.

    Database Upgrades & End-of-Support Source: AWS

  3. Licence cost

    VMware and database licence exits

    Renewal shocks are pushing teams to move workloads and databases to cloud-native and open-source engines.

    Cloud Migration & VMware Exit Source: CloudBolt research

  4. Cost pressure

    Cloud and AI spend under review

    Workload optimization and waste reduction lead the State of FinOps 2026, and AI cost is now in scope for most FinOps teams.

    FinOps & Cost Optimization Source: FinOps Foundation

  5. Pilot to production

    GenAI pilots that stalled

    Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be canceled by the end of 2027 on cost, value or risk. Production needs evaluation, guardrails and cost control.

    LLMOps, Evaluation & Guardrails Source: Gartner

  6. Budget shift

    AI budgets growing fast

    Gartner forecasts worldwide AI spending to grow 49.5% in 2026. Boards now expect production systems with measurable results, not more demos.

    RAG & Knowledge Assistants Source: Gartner

Questions, answered

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

What is the free 30-minute review?

A video call with a senior engineer about your databases, cloud platforms or AI plans. We cover what you run, which deadlines or cost issues affect you and what a sensible next step would be. You leave with a short written summary. No slides, no obligation.

How does the paid assessment work, and why is it paid?

Every assessment is fixed-scope and fixed-fee, scoped after the free 30-minute review and agreed in writing before work starts. It is paid because it is real engineering work, and the full fee is credited toward the project if you go ahead.

Which technologies do you cover?

Relational and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike, Redis, Valkey, DynamoDB, Cosmos DB), the four major clouds (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, OCI), Kubernetes and DevOps tooling, and AI stacks from vector databases to agents. See the platforms page for the full list.

Are you a partner of AWS, Microsoft, Google, Oracle or MongoDB?

No. We are independent. We do not resell licences or cloud capacity, so our recommendations are not tied to a commission.

How are projects scoped and billed?

Fixed fee and fixed scope, agreed in writing before work starts. Managed services have a fixed monthly fee. Changes are agreed in writing before they are made.

Who does the work?

A senior engineer on every engagement, from the first call to go-live.

Do you offer 24x7 support?

No. Managed services are business hours by default, with extended hours agreed in writing for specific windows such as a go-live.

How quickly will you reply?

Within one business day.
