About SRASH

An independent data, cloud and AI engineering firm.

SRASH helps organizations modernize their databases, build and run cloud platforms, and put AI into production. We are deliberately senior and deliberately independent: no resale margins, no junior hand-offs, and a fee agreed before we start.

Who we are

Senior engineers, not a pyramid.

SRASH is a small, independent firm, operated by SRASH Digital LLC and based in San Diego, California. We work remotely with teams in any time zone.

Every engagement is led and delivered by a senior engineer. We do not hand the work to juniors after the sale, and we do not resell licences or cloud capacity, so our recommendation follows the evidence rather than a margin.

Our work sits where data, cloud and AI meet. Database upgrades and migrations often need cloud and Kubernetes changes; AI assistants need vector search and well-run data platforms; cloud cost programs start with the databases. One firm across all three means one plan and one fee.

At a glance

  • Independent: no licence or cloud resale, no vendor partnerships
  • Four practices: databases, data engineering and analytics, cloud, and AI
  • AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI
  • Fixed-fee engagements, agreed in writing
  • Business-hours managed reliability
  • Based in San Diego, California

Who does the work

Senior engineers who own the outcome.

We introduce the engineer who would lead your work on the free review, and for embedded engagements you interview them before you commit.

Database engineering lead

Relational and NoSQL upgrades, migrations, performance and reliability across PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, MongoDB, Cassandra, Aerospike and Redis or Valkey.

Data platform and analytics lead

Lakehouses on Snowflake, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric, warehouse migrations, dbt and Airflow pipelines, Kafka streaming and CDC, BI and data governance.

Cloud and platform lead

Landing zones, migrations, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Terraform, observability and FinOps across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI.

AI engineering lead

Vector search, RAG, agents, GenAI applications, evaluation and LLMOps, built inside your cloud and model providers.

How we choose engineers

  • A decade or more of hands-on production experience in their specialty
  • Technical interview and a practical exercise led by a senior engineer
  • Writing sample: a plan or runbook a client could keep
  • Signed confidentiality, security and data-handling terms before any client work
