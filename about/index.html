About SRASH

An independent Oracle engineering firm, built for deadlines.

SRASH helps organizations upgrade, migrate and run Oracle middleware and databases on premises and on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI. We are deliberately focused: fewer services, done properly, at a price agreed before we start.

Who we are

Senior engineers, not a pyramid.

SRASH is a small, independent firm, operated by SRASH Digital LLC and based in San Diego, California. We work remotely with teams in any time zone.

Every engagement is led and delivered by a senior engineer. We do not hand the work to juniors after the sale, and we do not resell licences or cloud capacity, so our recommendation follows the evidence rather than a margin.

We focus on the work where Oracle estates are under real pressure right now: the end of Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12c, the retirement of Amazon RDS Custom for Oracle, database upgrades to 19c and 26ai, and the operations, modernization and AI work that follow.

At a glance

  • Independent: no licence or cloud resale
  • Oracle middleware and database specialists
  • AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI
  • Fixed-price engagements, agreed in writing
  • Business-hours managed services
  • Based in San Diego, California
