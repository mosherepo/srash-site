The 2-Week Data Estate Assessment

A senior engineer reviews your databases, middleware and cloud setup and gives you a written plan: what to keep, what to move, what it costs, what the deadlines mean for you, and a fixed price for the next step.

Request your assessment

What you get

  1. InventoryEvery database, middleware domain and key application, with version, edition, size and support date.
  2. Deadline exposure mapWhat is affected by the Dec 2026 Fusion Middleware and Mar 2027 RDS Custom deadlines, and when you need to start.
  3. Keep / move / modernize decisionA recommended target for each system (stay, rehost, re-platform, upgrade or retire), with the reasoning written down.
  4. Cost comparisonCurrent run cost against the recommended target, including licence position and cloud service pricing.
  5. Risk registerTechnical, data, security and schedule risks, each with a mitigation.
  6. Fixed price for step twoA scoped proof of concept or migration with a fixed price and dates. No obligation to accept it.

Timeline

  1. Day 1: 60-minute kickoff call. We agree scope and the exports we need.
  2. Days 2–3: You share inventory, AWR or pg_stat reports and configs. We send scripts that collect them read-only.
  3. Days 4–8: Analysis, target design, cost model and risk register.
  4. Day 9: Draft review with your team (about 45 minutes).
  5. Day 10: Final report and a fixed price for step two.

What we need from you

  • One technical contact, about 2 hours in total
  • Read-only exports (we provide the scripts)
  • Your NDA, if you have one

Good fit if…

  • You run Oracle on RDS Custom, EC2, on-premises or Exadata and need a decision before March 31, 2027
  • You run WebLogic, SOA, OSB, WebCenter or Forms & Reports on 12.2.1.4 or older
  • Your cloud database costs are rising and you want an independent second opinion
  • You want to use AI on your own data but need a safe, governed design first

Frequently asked questions

Is the 2-week assessment really free?

Yes, for a focused scope: one application estate or up to roughly 20 databases. You give us about two hours of your team’s time and read-only exports; we give you a written report you keep. Larger estates get a fixed-fee quote up front.

What do I get at the end of the assessment?

A written report with your inventory, support-deadline exposure, a keep / move / modernize decision for each system, a risk register, a cost comparison and a fixed price for the next step. It is yours whether or not you hire us.

Do you need access to our production systems?

No. We work from inventory exports, AWR or pg_stat reports and configuration files you share. Read-only access helps but is optional, and we sign your NDA.

How do you price projects?

Fixed price and fixed scope, agreed in writing before work starts. If we under-estimate, that is our problem, not yours.
