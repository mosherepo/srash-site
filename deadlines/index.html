Triggers without a date

Licence, cost and AI pressures.

Not deadlines, but just as likely to land on your desk this year.

Licence change

Redis to Valkey

Redis moved to source-available licences in 2024, then added AGPLv3 in Redis 8. Valkey, the Linux Foundation fork, stays BSD and is priced lower on ElastiCache.

Redis, Valkey & Caching →Source: Redis

Extended-support fees

Paying to stay on old versions

Amazon RDS and Cloud SQL charge extended support on PostgreSQL and MySQL versions past their standard support date, and the charge rises over time.

FAQ

About these dates

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

How do you count down to month-only dates?

When a vendor publishes only a month, such as December 2026, we count to the last day of that month, which is also how Microsoft defines AKS month-only dates.

What does end of standard support usually mean?

It varies by vendor. AWS and Google Cloud move databases and Kubernetes to paid extended support; Microsoft and Oracle stop security fixes or offer paid extended programs; some services retire outright. Each row links to the vendor's own terms.

Our version is already past its date. Is that a problem?

Often it means paying extended-support fees or running without security fixes. The free 30-minute review will tell you what it costs to stay and what it takes to move.
