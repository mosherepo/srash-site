The challenge

Sound familiar?

  • Your WebLogic, SOA, OSB or Forms estate runs on 12.2.1.4 (12.2.1.19 for Forms & Reports) and Premier Support ends in December 2026.

FAQ

Questions about this solution

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

When exactly does WebLogic 12c support end?

Oracle states that Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12c (12.2.1.4, or 12.2.1.19 for Forms and Reports) concludes in December 2026, and Extended Support ends in December 2027. After that Oracle provides no new updates or security fixes for 12c.

Why 14.1.2 and not wait for the next release?

14.1.2 shipped in December 2024 and Oracle plans Premier Support through 2030 and Extended Support through 2033. It is the suite release you can install today with published support dates past 2027. Oracle's later releases have no published support dates yet.

Can we upgrade directly from 12.2.1.3 or 12.1.3?

Oracle documents the upgrade to 14.1.2 from 12.2.1.4. Older releases need a staged path or a clean 14.1.2 build; the readiness review tells you which carries less risk.

Is the readiness review really credited?

Yes. The full fee for the review is deducted from the fixed price of the upgrade if you go ahead, typically within 60 days of the quote.

Oracle mentioned Market Driven Support for 12c. Can we rely on that?

Oracle says it intends to offer a Market Driven Support program for 12.2.1.4 yearly beyond 2027, with scope and terms to be announced later. Until the terms exist, plan as if 12c fixes stop after December 2027.
