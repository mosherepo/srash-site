FAQ

Questions about this solution

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Do we have to rewrite everything?

No. Most business logic stays in PL/SQL. We move it into packages and rebuild the screens in APEX.

Should we upgrade Forms to 14.1.2 first?

If the move to APEX will run past December 2026, upgrading Forms to 14.1.2 first keeps you on a supported release while the waves run.
