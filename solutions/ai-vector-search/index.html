FAQ

Questions about this solution

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Do we need a separate vector database?

Often not. Oracle 26ai and PostgreSQL with pgvector store and search vectors next to your relational data, which keeps security and sync simple.

Is AI Vector Search an extra Oracle licence?

Oracle's Database FAQ states that advanced AI features such as AI Vector Search are included in 26ai at no additional charge. Check your own contract and edition with Oracle.
