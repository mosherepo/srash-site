What we deliver

Database health check

A fixed-price, 1 to 2 week review of configuration, security, backups, performance and capacity, with a prioritized fix list.

Performance tuning

AWR, ASH and pg_stat analysis, SQL and index tuning, and parameter changes, measured before and after.

Managed DBA retainer

Monitoring, patching, backup and restore tests, capacity planning and a named engineer. Business-hours or extended coverage by agreement.

Upgrade and patch programs

Oracle 19c to 23ai/26ai, PostgreSQL major versions, quarterly patch cycles.

Runbooks and on-call readiness

Written procedures so your own team can handle the common incidents.

How an engagement runs

  1. Free 2-week assessment. Inventory, decisions and a fixed price.
  2. Fixed-price proof of concept. Proven on your real data in 2–4 weeks.
  3. Fixed-price delivery. Rehearsed, reversible, documented.
  4. Optional operations retainer. A named engineer who knows your estate.

Configuration, security, backup and recovery, performance and capacity, ending in a prioritized fix list. It is fixed price and usually takes 1 to 2 weeks.

Oracle, PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server and the main NoSQL and cloud-native services on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI.
