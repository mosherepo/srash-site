Questions about oracle middleware

When does WebLogic 12.2.1.4 support end?

Oracle’s Fusion Middleware Statement of Direction says Premier Support for 12c (12.2.1.4) concludes in December 2026, with Extended Support ending in December 2027. 14.1.2 is planned for Premier Support through 2030.

Can Oracle Forms run on Kubernetes?

Oracle supports WebLogic, SOA, OSB, WebCenter and Identity Management on Kubernetes through the WebLogic Kubernetes Operator. We keep Forms and Reports on VMs and modernize around them.

Can we upgrade straight from 12.2.1.3 or 12.1.3 to 14.1.2?

Not directly. Oracle supports upgrades to 14.1.2 from 12.2.1.4 (and 12.2.1.19 for Forms). Older releases take a staged path or a fresh 14.1.2 build, and we plan whichever is less risky.
