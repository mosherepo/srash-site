How we work

Fixed fees, written plans and senior engineers.

Four ways to work with us, one process to start, and commitments we put in the contract.

Book a free 30-minute review

The process

Three steps. You can stop after any of them.

Every engagement, in every practice, starts the same way.

  1. 01

    Free · 30 minutes

    Architecture & deadline review

    A senior engineer looks at what you run, the dates and costs ahead, and tells you plainly what is urgent. You get a short written summary.

  2. 02

    Fixed fee · credited

    Paid assessment

    A written plan from read-only access or exports: approach, risks, timeline and a fixed quote. The full fee is credited toward the project if you go ahead.

  3. 03

    Fixed fee · fixed scope

    Delivery

    Rehearsed, reversible execution with runbooks, code and documentation handed over to your team.

Buy an outcome, a service or senior capacity.

Pick the model that matches how your organization buys. All four come with the same commitments.

Fixed-fee sprints

A defined outcome in 2–8 weeks, such as an upgrade, a landing zone or an agent prototype, for a fee agreed in writing.

  • Written scope and plan
  • Rehearsal and rollback included
  • Changes agreed before they are made

Best for deadlines and well-defined problems

Project delivery

Larger migrations, platforms and AI products delivered in fixed-fee phases, each with its own scope and acceptance criteria.

  • Phased, fixed-fee milestones
  • Weekly status and demo
  • Everything in your repository

Best for multi-month programs

Managed reliability

A named senior engineer runs your data or cloud platforms SRE-style for a fixed monthly fee.

  • Agreed SLOs and monitoring
  • Monthly operations and cost report
  • Business-hours cover by default

Best for teams without dedicated platform staff

Embedded senior engineers

Senior database, cloud, platform or AI engineers working inside your team, by the month.

  • Interview before you commit
  • Corp-to-corp contract
  • Weekly status and handover

Best for adding senior capacity fast

Principles

What we put in the contract.

If we cannot keep a promise, we do not make it.

Senior engineer on every job

The engineer on the first call is the engineer who does the work.

Fixed scope, fixed fee

Agreed in writing before work begins. Changes are agreed in writing before they are made.

A written plan you keep

Every assessment ends in a document with scope, risks, timeline and rollback, whether or not you hire us next.

Rehearsed, reversible changes

Every cut-over is rehearsed with timings and has a tested way back.

Your NDA, your repository

We sign your NDA before we see anything, and everything we build lives in your repositories.

One business day

The longest you will wait for a reply from us.

Questions about working with us

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Can we hire an engineer rather than buy a project?

Yes. Embedded senior engineers work inside your team by the month on a corp-to-corp contract. You interview them before you commit.

What happens if scope changes?

We agree the change in writing before doing it. Nothing is added to an invoice without your agreement.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, with teams in any time zone. On-site visits can be arranged for workshops or cut-overs.

Who owns the work?

You do. Code, Terraform, runbooks and documentation go into your repositories.

Do you offer 24x7 support?

No. Managed reliability is business hours by default, with extended hours agreed in writing for specific windows.
