Fusion Middleware

WebLogic 12c support ends in December 2026. Here is what changes and what to do

Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12c ends in December 2026, Extended Support a year later. What the dates mean and how to plan the move to 14.1.2.

By the SRASH team · November 15, 2026 · 5 min read

Key facts

  • Premier Support for Fusion Middleware 12c (12.2.1.4, or 12.2.1.19 for Forms & Reports) concludes in December 2026.
  • Extended Support ends in December 2027. After that, no new updates or security fixes.
  • Fusion Middleware 14.1.2 (December 2024) is planned for Premier Support through 2030 and Extended Support through 2033.
  • Oracle intends to offer a yearly Market Driven Support program for 12c beyond 2027; terms are not yet published.

What Oracle has said

Oracle's support notice is short and direct: Premier Support for Oracle Fusion Middleware 12c concludes in December 2026, followed by the end of Extended Support in December 2027. After these dates, Oracle says it will no longer provide updates or security fixes for this version, and it strongly encourages customers to begin planning and executing upgrades as soon as possible.

The dates cover the 12c Release 2 line that most estates run today: 12.2.1.4 for WebLogic Server, SOA Suite, OSB, WebCenter and Identity Management, and 12.2.1.19 for Forms and Reports.

Premier Support pays for new fixes and security patches. Extended Support costs extra and ends a year later. Sustaining Support adds no new fixes at all.

Why 14.1.2 is the target

Fusion Middleware 14.1.2 was released in December 2024 as the current suite release. Oracle's Statement of Direction says it plans to support 14.1.2 with Premier Support through 2030 and Extended Support through 2033. Oracle has announced later releases, but without published support dates, so 14.1.2 is the target you can install and budget for today.

What changes in 14.1.2

  • Java. WebLogic Server 14.1.2 is certified on JDK 17 and JDK 21. Applications built for Java 8 need to be checked for removed APIs and changed behaviour.
  • Console. The classic WebLogic Server Administration Console was removed in 14.1.2. Administration moves to the WebLogic Remote Console, which uses the management REST API and can also be hosted on 14.1.2 Administration Servers.
  • Upgrade path. Oracle documents the upgrade to 14.1.2 from 12.2.1.4. Estates on 12.2.1.3, 12.1.3 or older need a staged path or a clean build.
  • Tooling. Oracle ships new upgrade tools in 14.1.2, and WebLogic Deploy Tooling makes it practical to rebuild domains from a model rather than upgrade in place.

What about Market Driven Support?

Oracle says it intends to offer a Market Driven Support program for 12.2.1.4 and 12.2.1.19 on a yearly basis beyond 2027, with scope, terms and availability to be communicated later. Treat it as a possible safety net, not a plan: until the terms are published you cannot know the price or what it covers.

A realistic timeline

A single WebLogic or Forms estate usually takes several weeks from readiness review to production, longer with SOA Suite or many environments. Working back from December 2026, the review should start now, with non-production upgrades early in the new year.

  1. Inventory every domain, product, patch level, JDK and database dependency.
  2. Choose the path per domain: in-place upgrade, staged upgrade or clean 14.1.2 build.
  3. Test on JDK 17 or 21 early, since that is where most surprises are.
  4. Rehearse the upgrade in non-production, with timings and a rollback plan.
  5. Upgrade production environment by environment.
