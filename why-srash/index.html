Where we fit

Best when the problem is specific and the stakes are real.

A dated upgrade, a migration, a cost problem, a stalled AI pilot, or a platform that needs a senior owner. Work that benefits from one accountable engineer, a written plan and a fee agreed up front.

Problems we solve

When a large firm is the better choice

  • You need around-the-clock global operations or a 24x7 service desk
  • The program needs dozens of people at once
  • You must buy through a vendor's partner tier or funding program
  • Your procurement requires a supplier with SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestation

If that is you, we will say so on the free review.

What you can hold us to.

  1. Senior engineer on every job

    The person on the first call does the work.

  2. Fixed fee

    Agreed in writing before work starts.

  3. Written plan

    Scope, risks, timeline and rollback, in a document you keep.

  4. Your NDA, signed

    Before we see anything about your systems.

  5. One business day

    The longest you will wait for a reply.

No invented statistics, borrowed logos or partner badges.
