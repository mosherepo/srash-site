Trust

Security and confidentiality, in plain terms.

How we access your systems, handle your data and use AI, written as commitments we can keep. We hold no security certifications and do not claim any; we work inside your controls.

Book a free 30-minute review

Access

  • Read-only first: assessments use read-only roles or exports wherever possible
  • Least privilege: write access only for agreed delivery work, scoped to the systems in the statement of work
  • Named, individual accounts with MFA, never shared logins
  • Time-bound access that you grant and revoke through your own identity provider
  • Your VPN, bastion or privileged-access tooling, under your logging

Data handling

  • Data stays in your environment; no copies of production data on our laptops or systems
  • Masked or synthetic data for testing when real data is not essential
  • Secrets stay in your secret manager; we never put credentials in email, chat or code
  • Working material returned or deleted at the end, confirmed in writing

AI and your data

  • No client data used to train models, ours or anyone else's
  • AI tools used on client material only when you approve the tool and provider
  • GenAI systems we build run in your cloud tenancy and model accounts
  • Prompts, retrieval and agent actions logged so you can audit them

Change safety

  • Every production change is in a written plan you approve
  • Rehearsed end to end, with measured timings
  • A tested rollback path for every cut-over
  • Infrastructure and configuration changes as reviewed pull requests

Contracts and confidentiality

  • We sign your NDA before we see anything about your systems
  • Confidentiality and data-handling terms in every statement of work
  • A data processing agreement on request
  • Engineers on every engagement are bound by the same terms

If something goes wrong

  • We tell you promptly, and within one business day, if we become aware of a security issue affecting your systems or data
  • We help you investigate and contain it, and document what happened
  • We follow your incident process when you have one

Vendor security review

Questionnaires and evidence

We complete your supplier security questionnaire, Shared Assessments SIG Lite or the Cloud Security Alliance CAIQ, answering only what we can truthfully commit to. Where a control is yours (for example your identity provider, VPN or logging), we say so rather than claim it.

Vendor onboarding pack

Available on request

  • Completed security questionnaire
  • Certificate of insurance
  • Data processing agreement; BAA review where health data is in scope
  • Mutual NDA and data-handling terms for every engineer
  • Written confirmation of access removal and data deletion at close

We hold no SOC 2, ISO 27001 or other security certification.

Security questions

If your security team has a questionnaire, send it with your request and we will complete it.

Ask a question

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

No. We are a small firm and do not hold those certifications. We work inside your controls, follow the practices on this page and complete your security questionnaire truthfully.

Will you use our data to train AI models?

No. We do not use client data to train models, and we only use AI tools on client material when you have approved the tool and the provider for that data.

Where does our data live during the engagement?

In your environment. We work through your accounts, VPN or bastion, and do not copy production data to our own systems unless you agree to it in writing for a specific purpose.

What happens at the end of the engagement?

You revoke our access, we return or delete any working material you shared, and we confirm deletion in writing.

Have a security questionnaire?

Send it with your request. We answer only what we can truthfully commit to, and sign your NDA first.
