Contracting

From NDA to kickoff, in order.

Paperwork runs in parallel with planning, so work can usually start within one to two weeks of a signed SOW, depending on your approvals and access.

  1. 01

    Mutual NDA

    Signed before we see anything about your systems. Yours or ours.

  2. 02

    Free 30-minute review

    A senior engineer, not a salesperson. A short written summary follows.

  3. 03

    MSA

    Commercial and legal terms once: liability, IP, confidentiality, insurance, termination. Your paper or ours.

  4. 04

    SOW per engagement

    Scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, schedule and fixed fee, approved before work starts.

  5. 05

    Vendor onboarding

    W-9, insurance certificate, security questionnaire and bank verification, run in parallel with the SOW.

  6. 06

    Kickoff

    Named engineer, access plan, cadence and escalation path agreed in week one.

Commercial terms

How we contract, without the fine print.

Fees are fixed and stated in each SOW after the free review. We do not publish prices, because scope drives the fee. Sample SOW outline

Engagement models

Fixed-fee sprints, phased fixed-fee projects, managed reliability by the month, or embedded senior engineers. No percentage-of-savings deals. Compare models

Change control

Changes to scope, schedule or fee are agreed in a signed change request before the work is done. Nothing appears on an invoice you did not approve.

Acceptance

Each deliverable has written acceptance criteria. You review and sign off; anything that misses the criteria is corrected within the fee.

Intellectual property

Deliverables, code and documentation are yours on payment, in your repositories. We keep only pre-existing tools and general know-how.

Subcontracting

None without your written approval and named individuals, all under the same terms.

Confidentiality and data

NDA first, data stays in your environment, no client data used to train AI models. Security practices

Liability and insurance

Termination and transition

Notice periods in the MSA. On exit we hand over work in progress, revoke access and confirm deletion in writing.

Business case

Measures you can take to your CFO.

We help you build the case from your own numbers: the cost of doing nothing (extended-support fees, incidents, idle spend), the cost of the work and the measures we will report against. We do not use invented benchmarks or other clients' results.

Data & Databases

  • Support exposure removed: Instances moved off end-of-support versions, and the extended-support fees avoided, calculated from the vendor's published rates and your own inventory.
  • Downtime per change: Planned downtime measured in rehearsal, then reported against the actual window after each cut-over.
  • Recovery proven: Restore time and data-loss window (RTO and RPO) tested, not assumed, and written into the runbook.
  • Performance against baseline: p95 latency, throughput and resource use for your critical queries, before and after.

Cloud & Platform Engineering

  • Spend against baseline: Monthly cloud spend by team or product against the baseline agreed at the start, from your billing export.
  • Cost allocation: The share of spend that is tagged and allocated to an owner.
  • Delivery performance: Deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate and time to restore (the DORA measures), before and after.
  • Support status: Clusters, node images and managed services inside standard support, tracked against published dates.

All Cloud & Platform Engineering measures

AI & Automation

  • Quality against threshold: Score on an evaluation set built with your experts, against a pass mark agreed before building.
  • Groundedness: The share of answers that cite the right source, measured on the evaluation set.
  • Cost per task: Model and infrastructure cost per answer or per completed task, with daily limits.
  • Time saved: Time per task measured with real users in a pilot, not estimated from a vendor benchmark.

Security and compliance

What we do, and what we do not claim.

We hold no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 attestation and do not claim any. We work inside your environment and controls, so access stays under your own identity provider, logging and approvals.

Questions IT leaders and procurement ask

Straight answers on contracts, risk and governance. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Are you SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?

No, and we do not claim to be. We work inside your environment and controls, complete your security questionnaire truthfully and put our data-handling commitments in the contract. If your policy requires an attested supplier for this scope, we will tell you on the first call.

Can you work on our paper?

Yes. We are happy to review your NDA, MSA and SOW templates. If you prefer, we provide a short mutual NDA, MSA and SOW for your legal team to mark up.

Who owns the deliverables?

You do, on payment: code, infrastructure definitions, runbooks and documentation. We keep only general know-how and tools we had before the engagement.

Do you subcontract?

Only with your written approval, naming the engineer in advance. Anyone working on your systems signs the same confidentiality, security and data-handling terms.

What if the engineer leaves or is unavailable?

Everything lives in your repositories and the RAID log from day one, so the work does not depend on one person's memory. A replacement engineer is introduced for your approval, and on longer engagements we can name a backup engineer at the start.

How do we exit?

Either party can end an engagement on the notice set in the MSA. You pay for work accepted to date, we hand over everything in progress, revoke our access and confirm deletion of working material.

Can you provide references?

We do not publish client names or logos. Where clients have agreed to it, reference conversations can be arranged during procurement. A paid assessment, credited to the project, is the lowest-risk way to judge our work directly.

How do you handle conflicts of interest?

We resell no licences or cloud capacity and take no referral fees from vendors, so recommendations follow your evidence. If a conflict arises, we disclose it in writing.
