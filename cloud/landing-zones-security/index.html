Cloud Landing Zones, Security & Governance

Most cloud problems start with a weak foundation: shared accounts, flat networks, admin keys on laptops and no audit trail. We design and build landing zones that let teams move fast inside guardrails, as code you own.

Book a free 30-minute review →See pricing

The challenge

Sound familiar?

  • Everything runs in one or two accounts or subscriptions, and nobody is sure who can change what.
  • A security questionnaire or SOC 2 audit asked for logging, encryption and least-privilege evidence you cannot produce quickly.
  • You are adding a second cloud and want the same controls on both.
  • Networking grew by hand and now blocks every new project.

What you receive

  • Current-state findings and target design
  • Terraform modules for the landing zone
  • Identity and guardrail policies
  • Security baseline evidence pack
  • Runbooks and handover sessions

Scope

What we deliver

Scope is confirmed in writing in the assessment, with anything out of scope listed too.

Account and subscription structure

AWS Organizations and Control Tower, Azure management groups, Google Cloud folders and OCI compartments, designed for your teams.

Identity and access

Single sign-on, role-based access, break-glass procedures and no long-lived keys.

Networking

Hub-and-spoke or shared VPC/VNet, private connectivity, DNS and egress control.

Guardrails

Service control policies, Azure Policy, organization policies and tagging enforced as code.

Logging and security baseline

Central audit logs, threat detection, encryption defaults and posture management turned on.

Everything as code

Terraform or OpenTofu modules, pipelines and documentation in your repository.

FAQ

Questions about this service

Straight answers on scope, price and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Do you use the providers' reference architectures?

Yes, as a starting point: AWS Control Tower, Azure Landing Zones, Google Cloud's foundation blueprint and the OCI Landing Zone. We adapt them to your size rather than deploy everything.

Can you work in our existing accounts?

Yes. Most work is incremental, moving existing workloads into a better structure step by step.

Will this make us compliant?

It gives you the controls and evidence most frameworks ask for. Certification itself is between you and your auditor.

Is the review fee credited?

Yes, in full, toward the build if you go ahead.
