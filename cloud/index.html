How we deliver Cloud & Platform Engineering work

The same five phases on every engagement in this practice, so you always know what happens next and what you will hold at the end of each phase.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Accounts, subscriptions, clusters, pipelines and the bill, through read-only roles and billing exports.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Security baseline, reliability, cost drivers and support dates measured against well-architected practice.

  3. 03

    Design

    Target architecture and a backlog as Terraform, GitOps and pipeline changes, with a fixed quote.

  4. 04

    Build and migrate

    Delivered as pull requests in your repositories, reviewed and reversible.

  5. 05

    Operate and hand over

    Runbooks, dashboards and alerts your team can use, plus an optional managed reliability service.

Questions about Cloud & Platform Engineering

Straight answers on scope, fees and how we work. Anything else, ask us.

Ask a question

Which clouds do you work with?

AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, plus Kubernetes on all of them and on premises.

Are you an AWS, Microsoft or Google partner?

No. We are independent and do not resell cloud capacity. That means we cannot offer provider funding programs that require partner status, but our advice is not tied to any provider.

Do you build with Terraform?

Yes, Terraform or OpenTofu by default, committed to your repository so you own and can change everything.

Can you help reduce our cloud bill?

Yes. The FinOps cost review is fixed fee, and we do not take a percentage of savings.
