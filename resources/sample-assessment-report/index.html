Sample assessment report: database upgrade readiness

The structure and depth of the written plan you receive at the end of a paid assessment, shown with fictional systems.

Sample / template. Fictional systems and figures that show the format and depth of what you receive. Not a client deliverable.

Prepared for: Example Co. (fictional) · Scope: 6 PostgreSQL and 2 MongoDB databases on AWS · Length of a real report: typically 15–30 pages

1. Executive summary

Four of six PostgreSQL instances run version 13 or 14 and will move to paid Extended Support when standard support ends. Two MongoDB replica sets run 6.0, which is past end of life. We recommend upgrading in three waves over six weeks, starting with the lowest-risk reporting databases. The fixed price for delivery is in section 6.

3. Findings and risk register

#FindingImpactRecommendation
R1Extension pg_example pinned to an old releaseBlocks upgradeUpgrade extension in a prior change
R2No restore tested in the last 12 monthsUnknown recovery timeRestore test before wave 1
R3MongoDB drivers on two services too old for 8.0Application errorsDriver upgrade in application sprint

4. Upgrade approach

  • PostgreSQL: RDS blue/green deployments, rehearsed per wave, with measured switchover
  • MongoDB: rolling upgrade 6.0 to 7.0 to 8.0, one member at a time
  • Rollback: switch back to blue environment or restore from snapshot, both rehearsed

5. Plan and timeline

WaveDatabasesWindowDuration

6. Fixed quote

A single fixed price for all three waves, including rehearsals, runbooks and hand-over, with the assessment fee credited. Changes to scope are priced in writing before they are made.

7. Assumptions and out of scope

  • Application testing is performed by your team within each window
  • Out of scope: schema redesign, new features and engines not listed above

Appendices

Full inventory export, query baselines, parameter differences, and the draft runbook for wave 1.
