How to start

From first message to delivery in about 30 days.

No long sales cycle and no free discovery that ends in a pitch. Here is exactly what happens, what we need from you and what you receive at each step.

Book a free 30-minute review →

The first 30 days

A typical timeline

Timings depend on your access and approvals. You can stop after any step and keep everything we have written.

  1. Day 1

    Request

    Send the form, email us or pick a time. We reply within one business day.

  2. Days 2–5

    Free 30-minute review

    A senior engineer reviews what you run and what is coming. We sign your NDA first if you need one.

  3. Within 2 business days of the call

    Written summary

    What is urgent, what can wait, and a fixed price for an assessment if one would help.

  4. Weeks 1–3

    Paid assessment

    Discovery from read-only access or exports, ending in a written plan with risks, timeline and a fixed quote.

  5. Around day 30

    Delivery starts

    Fixed-price delivery against the plan, or managed reliability, or an embedded engineer.

What we need from you

Small asks, read-only first.

Most assessments need a few hours of your team's time. See security and confidentiality for how we handle access.

Data & Databases

  • List of engines, versions and sizes (or read-only access to collect it)
  • Read-only database credentials or exports, such as pg_stat_statements, AWR or MongoDB diagnostics
  • Backup and DR approach as it is today
  • Business windows and any hard dates

Cloud & Platform Engineering

  • A read-only role in the relevant accounts, subscriptions or projects
  • Billing export access for cost work
  • Cluster and pipeline list, with repository access for review
  • Security and compliance requirements you must meet

AI & Automation

  • The use case, its users and what good looks like
  • A representative sample of documents or data, in your environment
  • Your approved model providers and cloud
  • A subject-matter expert for a few hours to build the evaluation set

Engagement models

Buy an outcome, a service or senior capacity.

Pick the model that matches how your organization buys. All four come with the same commitments.

Fixed-price sprints

A defined outcome in 2–8 weeks, such as an upgrade, a landing zone or an agent prototype, for a price agreed in writing.

  • Written scope and plan
  • Rehearsal and rollback included
  • Changes priced before they are made

Best for deadlines and well-defined problems

Project delivery

Larger migrations, platforms and AI products delivered in fixed-price phases, each with its own scope and acceptance criteria.

  • Phased, fixed-price milestones
  • Weekly status and demo
  • Everything in your repository

Best for multi-month programs

Managed reliability

A named senior engineer runs your data or cloud platforms SRE-style for a fixed monthly fee.

  • Agreed SLOs and monitoring
  • Monthly operations and cost report
  • Business-hours cover by default

Best for teams without dedicated platform staff

Embedded senior engineers

Senior database, cloud, platform or AI engineers working inside your team, by the month.

  • Interview before you commit
  • Corp-to-corp contract
  • Weekly status and handover

Best for adding senior capacity fast

FAQ

Getting started, procurement and contracts

The questions buyers and procurement teams ask before the first call.

What is the smallest engagement?

The free 30-minute review. After that, the smallest paid engagement is a fixed-price assessment, and its fee is credited to the project if you go ahead.

How quickly can you start?

The free review is usually within a few business days of your request. Paid work typically starts within one to two weeks of a signed statement of work, depending on scope and access.

Do we need to give you production access?

Not for the free review or most assessments. Read-only access or exports are usually enough. Write access is only requested for agreed delivery work, scoped and time-bound.

What contracts do you use?

A mutual NDA, a short master services agreement and a statement of work per engagement with the scope, deliverables, price and acceptance criteria. We are happy to review your paper instead.

Can you complete our vendor onboarding and security questionnaire?

Yes. We complete supplier forms and security questionnaires as part of onboarding, and answer only what we can truthfully commit to.

How do you invoice?

Fixed-price assessments are invoiced on delivery of the written plan. Delivery projects are invoiced by milestone. Managed reliability and embedded engineers are invoiced monthly.

Can we talk to the engineer who would do the work?

Yes. The senior engineer on the free review is the person who would lead the work, and for embedded engagements you interview the engineer before you commit.

What if we only want the plan?

That is fine. The assessment ends in a written plan you keep, whether or not you hire us for delivery.
